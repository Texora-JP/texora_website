A strong direction for TEXORA would be a modern Japanese technology aesthetic rather than a traditional “Japan-themed” website. The goal should be to feel unmistakably Japanese through restraint, spacing, typography, rhythm, and subtle cultural references—without relying heavily on cherry blossoms, torii gates, or stereotypical imagery.

Design Concept: “Modern Japan, Global Technology”

The visual identity should combine three ideas:

Japanese precision — clean grids, careful spacing, minimal clutter
Friendly technology — bright surfaces, soft shapes, approachable illustrations
Global readiness — bilingual content, modern SaaS layout, strong portfolio presentation

The site should feel like a Japanese engineering studio that is technically serious but easy to work with.

1. Overall visual direction

Use a spacious layout with a lot of white space in Light mode and deep charcoal surfaces in Dark mode.

The design language should use:

thin borders
rounded cards
subtle shadows
large typography
clean geometric layouts
occasional Japanese-inspired line patterns
soft animations rather than flashy effects

A good visual reference is the balance between a Japanese editorial website and a modern SaaS landing page.

Avoid making the design too corporate. TEXORA should feel like a small, skilled engineering team, not a large consulting company.
